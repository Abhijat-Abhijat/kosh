# Code of Conduct

Kosh adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)** as its code of conduct. It applies to everyone in all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and any other channel run by the project, and to anyone representing the project in public.

## Our pledge (summary)

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Examples of behaviour that helps: empathy and kindness, respecting different opinions, giving and accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility for mistakes. Examples of unacceptable behaviour: sexualised language or imagery, trolling, insults, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, publishing others' private information without permission, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

The [full text](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) is the binding version, including the enforcement guidelines (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban).

## Reporting

We don't publish a personal email address. To report a problem privately:

- Use GitHub's **[Report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)** option on the comment, issue or user, which goes to GitHub and the maintainers; or
- Open a private report through **[Security Advisories](https://github.com/Abhijat-Abhijat/kosh/security/advisories/new)** and say it is a conduct report. Only the maintainers can see it.

If neither fits, open an issue asking a maintainer to contact you, without any details of the incident.

Maintainers will review every report promptly and fairly and will respect the privacy of the reporter.
