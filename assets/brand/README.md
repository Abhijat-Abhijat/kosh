# Kosh brand

*Kosh* means treasury, a kept store of value. The mark is a lowercase **k** made from the app's own parts. A solid stem is the store. Thin vertical lines, like the app's line-bar charts, form the two arms. The upper arm rises like a growing chart.

## Files

| File | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `logo-mark.svg` | App-style tile: paper symbol on a forest square. Avatars, favicons, social profiles. |
| `logo-symbol.svg` | Symbol only, ink, transparent background. |
| `logo-mark-mono.svg` | Symbol only, `currentColor`. Use it for one-colour work: monochrome icons, embossing, single-ink print. |
| `logo-wordmark.svg` | Symbol plus "kosh" in ink, on light backgrounds. The lettering is converted to paths, so no font is needed. |
| `logo-wordmark-light.svg` | Same, in paper colour, for dark backgrounds. |
| `social-card.png` | 1280×640, GitHub social preview and Open Graph image. |
| `feature-graphic.png` | 1024×500, store listings (F-Droid, IzzyOnDroid, Play). |

The app icons in `assets/images/` and `assets/expo.icon/` use the same symbol. On Android adaptive icons the symbol sits well inside the central 66% safe zone, on a paper (`#C3C6B0`) background.

## Clear space and size

- Keep clear space around the mark equal to the width of the solid stem, on every side.
- Smallest sizes: symbol 16 px, wordmark 72 px wide. Below 16 px, use the symbol alone.
- Don't place the symbol tighter than 12% padding inside a square tile.

The mark appears in **ink on paper**, **paper on forest**, or one colour. Orange and yellow are signal colours, never logo colours.

## Type

- **Familjen Grotesk** for display and text. Headlines pair a light, muted lead line with a bold line: *Your money,* / **kept.**
- **Chakra Petch** for numbers. The decimal part is faded: ₹1,84,320<span style="opacity:.45">.0</span>.

Both are from Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Familjen Grotesk is by Familjen STHLM. Chakra Petch is by Cadson Demak. The wordmark lettering is Familjen Grotesk Bold converted to outlines. On the web, fall back to `"Helvetica Neue", Arial, system-ui, sans-serif` and `"DIN Alternate", Bahnschrift, ui-monospace, monospace`.

## Do

- Use flat colour. Full-bleed bands, thin vertical lines, dark square tiles.
- Keep the stem and lines exactly as drawn. Scale the whole mark, never parts of it.

## Don't

- Don't add gradients, shadows, bevels, glows or outlines.
- Don't round the stem or the lines, or change their spacing.
- Don't rotate, stretch or recolour the mark in orange, yellow or blue.
- Don't set the wordmark in another typeface.
- Don't put the mark on a busy photo without a solid paper or forest panel behind it.

## Regenerating

The SVGs and icon PNGs were generated by a script from the geometry below, on a 96-unit grid:

- Stem: x 17, y 12, 20 × 72.
- Five lines, 4.5 wide, at x 41, 49.5, 58, 66.5 and 75. Each covers a 18-unit band (±9) centred on the upper arm (from y 58 at the stem up to y 30 at x 79) and the lower arm (from y 58 down to the baseline, y 84). Where the two bands meet, the line is one piece.
